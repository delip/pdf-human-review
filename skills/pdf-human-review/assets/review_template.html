How to comment

Select text to comment on it. For a figure, table or equation, press the area button and drag a box around it.

Reading aloud

Press the speaker button in the top bar to have the paper read to you, with the sentence and the word being spoken highlighted. Or right-click anywhere on a page and choose Read aloud from here. The same button pauses and resumes, the turtle button sets the reading speed, and the square button stops. The voice is generated on your computer. The first use downloads it, about 330 MB.

Moving through the pages

Scroll with the mouse wheel or trackpad, or use the page controls in the top bar: first, previous, next and last page. To jump to a page, type its number in the box and press Enter.

Links inside the PDF work: click a section number, a citation or a figure reference to go to it, then press the back arrow at the left of the toolbar, or B, to return. Like a browser's back button, it steps back through the links you followed, one at a time. Web links open in a new tab. Dragging across a link still selects its text.

Arranging the toolbar

Each group of buttons in the toolbar has a dotted handle on its left. Drag the handle to move the group, or select the handle and press the left and right arrow keys. The arrangement is remembered.
